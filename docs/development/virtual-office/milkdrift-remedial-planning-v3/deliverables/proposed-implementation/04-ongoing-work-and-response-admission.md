## Durable control and cumulative accounting

Implement the closed account origin and exact commitment-action/run association together. Establishing
one accepted commitment, its policy, account and first pending action is atomic. Do not fabricate a root
run to satisfy today's controller-shaped account fields. Current controller/publication account meanings
retain exact legacy readers and replay; arithmetic/reservation/settlement stays with one account owner.

Before runtime creation, retain the complete canonical internal command material and action identity.
The runtime create transaction checks accepted association, origin, account, exact authority, deadline
and admission fence, then creates and binds the real run atomically. Start is a separate exact ordinary
command. A persisted pause/cancel fence is checked atomically with owner-local durable entry acceptance.
An effect admitted before closure may physically begin afterward and remains subject to ordinary
cancellation/uncertainty. Traverse actual local published ancestors: an immediate PublishedInvocation
account can still descend from a controlling commitment. At a Serving boundary, the remote owner's
accepted facts govern. A cannot retroactively fence B's already accepted operation. Send exact remote
cancellation; B may enter its private child until B observes cancellation, deadline or changed authority.
A retains the unsettled operation and reservation through message loss. Remote/published work retains
its own actual authority/account scope and attributable outer usage;
the commitment cannot claim inspection or control of a service's private balance.

Every local planner/critic/work/evaluation run admitted as commitment work inherits its cumulative account.
A nested marked controller must meet the reviewed whole-allowance containment rule or refuse; do not invent
hierarchical partial balances. Unknown reservations remain held across reconsideration/restart. Existing
independent entered runs cannot be adopted into a fresh allowance. They can be inspected or prospectively
revised under separately shown authority/accounting, without pretending that they became commitment children.

Response consumption binds exact action/output once. Save exact proposal/apply/resume commands before
calling their owners. After underlying acceptance but lost report, recover/query/replay that same receipt;
do not repeat the model or reconstruct different command bytes under its key. A target changing during
planning causes retained stale refusal, not silent rebase. A paused nonterminal target can be reconsidered
because control observes its actual owner facts, rather than waiting for a Call to return terminal output.

The bounded continuation driver operates on persisted pending actions in the existing daemon composition.
It starts ordinary runs and consumes accepted results; it performs no provider work inline and holds no
unbounded callback queue. Stop closes new admission, requests actual cancellations and exposes unsettled
work/holds separately. Answers bind their exact outstanding question and expected commitment version.
Restart cannot invent an answer or silently turn a timeout into approval.
