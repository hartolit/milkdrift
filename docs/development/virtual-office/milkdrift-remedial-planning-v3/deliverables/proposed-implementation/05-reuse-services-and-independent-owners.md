# P05 — Reuse and operate work across independent authorities

Owner: public service/reuse boundary engineer and workbench consumer. Requires P04, P1/r3 and
approved A1/F1/N1 for V04–V06/V08/V11/V12. Read [shared context](context.md), source traces for
subworkflow/copy/publication/direct/peer operations, `SavedRunRequest`, serving catalog/acceptance,
control publication and their real consumers/tests. Retain P1's independent dissent about discovery.

Implement shared discovery with explicit commit actions and distinct direct routes. A selected
exact workflow reference executes at its workflow owner; an eligible copy creates independent
definition identity/provenance without copying runs, grants or mutable state. Identity-bound
governed copy refuses with a useful alternative. A readable remote revision does not become a
local pin or silently publish a service. Existing import/copy possibilities require explicit source,
destination, rights and daemon validation. No generic call registry or global method identity is added.

Implement publication preparation/review/publish/inspect/generation/retire and invoke-only public
call/result workflows. Show current generation versus accepted pin and private internals versus
public contract. Close R-A04 with a bounded authorized invocation→internal run/revision association
read derived from the existing publication association. Existing run `published_source` is the
reverse view, not a complete caller-to-internal discovery operation. Require independently granted
internal read permission; unauthorized callers receive no hidden identifiers or existence hints.
No browser joins private ledgers. The reopened R-A05 finding is a contract distinction: retain exact
ListMethods/InspectMethod administrative snapshot receipts, add current per-record-authorized bounded
management queries with opaque scoped cursors/truthful continuation, and use the existing filtered
capability catalog for invocation. Do not weaken administration rights to simulate public discovery.

Internal IDs, titles and relationship metadata remain private even when attached to a public call.
On grant/session change clear them from authorized views and reject late old-session replies;
recheck independent read rights before expansion. Preserve public invocation progress when internal
disclosure disappears. Do not promise erasure of material previously exported by the user.

Add direct invocation input preparation/upload/submit/lookup/observation/output/cancel on the
selected host, including execution-only C. Direct work receives no fake workflow ID/context.
Recovery follows its actual operation owner: unlike native SavedRunRequest's exact-authority check,
the direct serving retained-request path can use current Inspect permission and the stored original
acceptance basis to validate exact replay. The client never rewrites that basis. Test an explicitly
initiated changed-grant direct recovery that the owner permits, and denied inspection/conflicting
bytes; do not blanket-ban permitted direct replay or generalize it to workflow commands.
A workflow at A may select exact granted peer operations at B through existing placement, with
the original workflow account/provenance. Merely adding B to the client creates no peer relationship.
Expose a useful boundary error, not automatic trust/setup or unsafe failover. No direct C→B relay
or workflow migration is included; these remain separately scoped excluded extensions.

Exercise three real owner identities, two workflows named release, same owner at two addresses,
different actors/grants, replacement host at old address, stale generation and revoked rights.
Alias views may converge only after authenticated identical owner and compatible auth context;
authority is never unioned. Late replies cannot change another connection's draft/run/cursor.
An accepted call keeps its owner/identity during disconnect, retirement and reconnect. Unknown
entry never becomes a new effect by switching hosts.

Acceptance: author lacking publication administration can use permitted local composition;
invoke-only customer obtains declared result and cannot read internal graph; newly granted internal
read reveals a link without making the service editable; restricted lists make permitted progress
without disclosing forbidden rows; lost accept/result/promotion replies and restart preserve one
child and exact generation; direct and workflow-origin calls preserve distinct records/accounts.
Run publication/recovery/retirement/constraints, direct/reuse/roles/two-daemon tests and real workbench
journeys. Test awkward Unicode names and reversed completion order. Existing E02/E03 is a baseline,
not a substitute for changed code/browser acceptance.

Treat new DTO/version fields atomically across protocol, daemon, Rust client/CLI, workbench and
fixtures. Preserve accepted association/storage meaning; add no duplicate durable mapping.
Commit public relationship/discovery contract with consumers, then connected action paths, then
cross-owner/refusal/recovery corrections. Stop with complete usable ordinary and restricted paths;
do not include third-host relay or a global transaction under convenience wording.
