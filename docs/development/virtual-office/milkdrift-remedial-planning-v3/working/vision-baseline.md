# Working goals — G1/r1, P1/r2

This derived baseline points to [G1/r1](../../whiteboard/discussions/vision/remedial-purpose.md)
and [P1/r2](../../whiteboard/discussions/product-model/reuse-service-and-placement.md), selected
by Rowan for planning after independent source intake, opposing positions, source investigation,
bounded observations and fresh-reader challenge. It does not amend canonical vision.

Milkdrift serves people and agents who need an inspectable process for evolving work rather than
manual prompt shuffling. The useful result is a method they can run with new inputs, understand,
change prospectively and evaluate for reuse, with explicit authority over tools and independent
machines. A real standalone Svelte client must expose ordinary and demanding work through public
daemon operations. Its first connected slice should arrive early enough to challenge this model.

Retain direct tools, controlled adaptation, branch/wait/result semantics, exact reuse, restricted
service calls, persistent managed resources, evidence/knowledge, evaluated promotion and honest
recovery. These are valued current abilities under I1, not all newly confirmed user requirements.
Question their current shape. Do not manufacture simplicity by removing difficult use or a second
execution engine by moving semantics into a frontend.

Concrete success: an author runs a method on two briefs; a reviewer finds a failed result; a
permitted future repair retains failed evidence; an independent service customer can invoke its
published version without seeing internal authority; another host supplies a permitted operation.
Concrete refusal: changing the deployment target or required verifier is not a repair inside an
unchanged agreement. Missing terminal evidence stays uncertain. Disconnect does not repeat work.

Conditional scope: generic text nodes, global static/dynamic modes, compulsory publication, fixed
Canvas/Timeline/Inspector navigation, full standards interchange and automatic cross-owner workflow
migration are not approved requirements. A copy of a governed identity may refuse. A revision
read at another owner does not become locally executable by drawing a call box.

03 can compare complete product representations now. 04–06 may iterate against these goals.
Shared discovery's comprehension, browser packaging, richer authoring usability, resource release
and the fit of governed adaptation to S28 require explicit proof or user choices in the final
program. User-approved standing is reserved for a future decision, never inferred from this baseline.
