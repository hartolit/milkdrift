# What value should survive the remedial redesign?

## Current decision — G1, revision 2

Selected for this planning baseline by Rowan-20261010 after the reopened investigation. U17/U18
approve the product directions below; they do not approve the proposed architecture or authorize
implementation. Milkdrift should let people and authorized agents develop, perform, inspect, revise
and reuse methods of work, including dependable acceptance and independent tools/owners. A real
standalone Svelte interface must expose that work. The justification is the qualified
[U01–U18 source](../../../milkdrift-remedial-planning-v3/intent-source-excerpts.md), especially
U01–U04, U07–U09 and U15–U18, with origin/standing separated in
[I1](../../../milkdrift-remedial-planning-v3/working/intent-register.md).

Retain existing useful direct, managed, publication, evaluation and recovery abilities while
comparing their representations. None earns permanent retention solely from tests or an ADR.
No evidence currently justifies deleting their useful outcomes. Conversely, do not turn every
seed aspiration into a mandatory new subsystem. Release from management, generalized workflow
migration, full standards interchange and guaranteed autonomous improvement remain unresolved or
unproven additions rather than hidden obligations.

The approved continuing-work outcome is goal → plan → execute → evaluate/review → reconsider →
revise → continue or stop within one identifiable durable commitment. A human need not manually
initiate every planning round. Criticism triggers assessment, which may retain the original result,
seek more evidence, revise, escalate or stop. Accepted evidence and disagreement remain traceable;
fresh contexts, retries, child work, remote delegation and restart do not reset authority or the
parent allowance. Manual operation remains supported. Repeated equivalent plans without new
accepted evidence reach an explicit finite stop or escalation rule. This is not guaranteed
improvement or permission to weaken the accepted goal, success conditions or protected checks.

Knowledge evaluation is not limited to executable methods or mechanical verification. An
authorized human may assess an exact explanation, architectural decision or research lesson under
stated criteria. The evidence retains inputs, versions/outputs, criteria, reviewer authority and
relationship, judgment, reasoning, limits and counterevidence. Self-review is permitted when
labeled unless an explicit declaration requires independence. Retrospective assessment remains
useful when labeled; only a predeclared comparison may claim that controlled basis. Human/agent
judgment and automated verification remain different evidence types. Evaluation does not adopt,
replace, publish or authorize anything by itself.

The reopened [agent-directed comparison](../../../milkdrift-remedial-planning-v3/working/agent-directed-work.md)
finds no current product goal-to-plan coordinator: supplied controller bodies and external evidence
drivers provide different parts of the demonstration. It also finds that current knowledge
selection/evaluation require managed protected methods. These are concrete reconstruction needs,
not reasons to discard their useful execution or protected-verification behavior.

Definition, accepted history and authority remain distinct in the chosen proposal because a
user must be able to change future work and explain earlier effects without giving an agent
permission to rewrite its checks. This is reasoned retention, not unquestioning acceptance of
the present vision. Typed nodes, screen taxonomy, managed mechanism and publication setup remain
open to replacement with a complete value and transition account.

Strongest objection: preservation can keep expensive mechanisms whose current demand is uncertain.
The response is to retain compatibility and visible access during this program, while testing
their combined purpose in S21/S26/S28. A proposed retirement needs a specific user tradeoff;
the present excerpts cannot establish consent to loss. No implementation branch assumes it.

Requires explicit planning scope and I1's source distinctions. Informs P1 reuse, representation,
architecture, notation, frontend and adoption. Reopen when a concrete user journey contradicts
the value account, a source clarifies demand, or the user selects a meaningful outcome loss.
Full original conversation and real frontend use remain missing evidence.

## Purpose questions

Can a novice start useful work without learning storage/runtime vocabulary while an expert can
still inspect its true constraints? Does dependable adaptation permit useful revision of the
method itself, or merely retries? Is each preserved mechanism valuable in a real combined case,
or only in an isolated fixture? What meaningful outcome would a proposed simplification remove?
