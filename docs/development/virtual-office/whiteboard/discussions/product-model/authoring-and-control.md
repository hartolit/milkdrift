where they eliminate duplicated multi-client construction rules; raw validated mutations remain
useful for agents and advanced clients.

validation, not an accepted graph that hangs. [E07](../../../milkdrift-remedial-planning-v3/working/observations.md)
and [standards evidence](../../../milkdrift-remedial-planning-v3/working/standards-evidence.md)

explicit selected-arm evidence. Its exact profile is reviewed with N1; until implemented it cannot
appear as a working ordinary merge. An explicit pinned-child alternative remains available but
adds a definition/run/context boundary that must be visible and cannot be silently inserted.

The semantic obligations from N1/r2 survive: normal arm exit differs from run completion/quiescence;
selected outputs have exact provenance; continuation becomes eligible atomically; and compatible
pending work inside a selected arm can be repaired. The selected new representation is a canonical
structured program with stable element IDs, ordered Conditional clauses and explicit results,

lowering produces the checked execution plan; the diagram is a projection. Generated ports/edges
are not a second editable source. Existing authority, remaining allowance and unresolved physical

The [complete structural comparison](../../../milkdrift-remedial-planning-v3/working/structure-comparison.md)

No human/model proficiency improvement is claimed without an actual comparative authoring test.

The [agent-directed comparison](../../../milkdrift-remedial-planning-v3/working/agent-directed-work.md)
also rejects caller-triggered planning as the capability ceiling. `WorkCommitment` owns accepted
ongoing intent, exact planning/action associations and total allowance before a method exists and
across its runs. The recommended coordinator belongs within control/controller; runtime remains
the only task/effect scheduler. Its actions use ordinary proposal/approval/adoption and run paths,
exact response provenance and a shared account with a durable owner-local admission fence.
Previously accepted remote work remains under its actual serving owner's cancellation/deadline
rules; stopping the commitment can leave remote entry and usage uncertain. Criticism is assessed
against actual evidence; it is not automatically accepted as a command to revise.

No-progress uses a bounded control-owned normal form of ordered structure/configuration/value
references and declared evidence roles/content/outcome keys. Generated identity, layout, timestamp
or reason-text changes do not themselves establish progress. Absolute round/time/allowance limits
remain even when the proxy observes a change; it is not a general equivalence or truth proof.

An extended root workflow with dynamically admitted child targets is a serious alternative. To
react while a child is paused/running it additionally needs asynchronous child ownership,
checkpoint observation, concurrent supervision and cancellation/drain semantics. The selected
control-owned commitment changes coordination intent without adding those lifetime rules to every
Call/Parallel consumer. The cost is a closed coordination policy: planner/critic methods remain
reusable workflows, but arbitrary supervisory algorithms are not falsely presented as ordinary
editable methods. One-round operation is a policy of the same owner, not another orchestrator.

`AmendCommitmentPolicy` accepts an authorized immutable successor with exact version/sequence and
action/frontier guards. Local admission pauses during the transition; explicit unaffected actions
may carry forward only under their original authority and obligations plus current checks. Affected
future work is held and replanned. Broader newly authorized work needs its own run basis. Original
aggregate account/time/round ceilings remain fixed; no policy change resets spent or unknown usage.

Knowledge assessment uses a separate closed single-subject or paired judgment shape. A reviewer
can judge one architectural lesson against an explicit criterion without inventing a baseline
workflow. Declared required judgments which conflict remain disputed; missing required evidence
remains inconclusive. Separate authorized reuse may retain a bounded caution or open question,
but cannot turn those judgments into favorable verification or silently erase disagreement.

Historical graph definitions, runs, attempts, proposals and receipts keep exact interpretation.
New writers emit structured source; version-specific readers lower old source into the same checked
plan. Prospective conversion must prove its active/pending mapping and retain uncertainty, holds
and accounts. Unsupported conversion refuses before effects and preserves the old accepted run;
it does not erase history or silently run a second scheduler.

Strongest remaining objections: a valued graph may not decompose without changing semantics, and
an authored supervisor may need capabilities beyond the closed commitment policy. Preserve these
countercases explicitly; do not add a raw-graph escape hatch or hidden client supervisor to claim
completion. Each new source/commitment operation must show one construction/admission owner and
an exact recovery path. This choice is based on the worked consequences, not rewrite size.

second method? Can they distinguish synchronizing branches from judging their outputs? Does a

graph would a region-first replacement lose, and is that loss justified?
