Milkdrift is the name used by Benjamin Hoffmeyer for the official Milkdrift software project.

This policy explains how the Milkdrift name, logo, and related brand assets may be used. It does not claim that Milkdrift is a registered trademark, and it does not create rights beyond those provided by applicable law.

The following are treated as Milkdrift brand assets:

* the Milkdrift name when used to identify the official project;

## Permitted references

Without separate permission, you may:

* truthfully refer to Milkdrift;
* link to the official Milkdrift repository or website;
* state that another project or product is compatible with Milkdrift;
* state that another project or product is based on, derived from, or built using Milkdrift;
* use the Milkdrift name when discussing, reviewing, documenting, or reporting on the project;
* include reasonable screenshots or images of the official software when discussing or documenting it; and
* redistribute an unmodified official Milkdrift release with its original name, branding, license, and notices intact.

These uses must not falsely suggest that another person, project, product, or service is official, sponsored, maintained, endorsed, approved, or operated by Milkdrift or Benjamin Hoffmeyer.

## Forks and modified distributions

The Apache License 2.0 permits forks and modified distributions of the software.

A modified distribution may truthfully describe itself using wording such as:

* “Based on Milkdrift”;
* “Derived from Milkdrift”; or
* “Built using Milkdrift.”

The modified distribution should use its own primary name and visual identity. Its own name should be more prominent than the reference to Milkdrift.

This policy does not authorize a modified distribution to:

* present itself as the official Milkdrift project;
* use the Milkdrift logo as its own product or project logo;
* use Milkdrift as its primary product or service name;
* use a name or visual identity intended to create confusion with the official Milkdrift project; or
* imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, approval, or official status.

A fork does not need permission merely to use, modify, redistribute, or commercially exploit the Apache-licensed software.

## Domains, accounts, and application listings

This policy does not authorize use of the Milkdrift name or logo in a domain name, social-media account, package identity, application-store listing, company name, organization name, or service name in a manner likely to make it appear official.

Truthful identifiers that clearly describe compatibility or origin are allowed when they do not create confusion. For example:

* `example-for-milkdrift`;
* `milkdrift-integration`;
* “Example, based on Milkdrift.”

The surrounding presentation must make clear that the offering is independently maintained when that is the case.

## Logo use

The Milkdrift logo may be used without separate permission only when reasonably necessary to:

* identify or link to the official Milkdrift project;
* discuss, review, or document Milkdrift;
* show an unmodified official Milkdrift release; or
* accurately describe compatibility with Milkdrift.

The logo must not be altered or used as the primary logo of another product, project, service, or organization without prior written permission.
